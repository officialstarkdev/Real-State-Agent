About James Harrington

James Harrington has spent twelve years guiding buyers, sellers and investors through some of the world's most competitive property markets. What began in Sydney's harbourside suburbs has grown into a truly international practice spanning Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom and the Gulf region.

James specialises in high-net-worth clients — expatriates, investors and relocating families who need a single trusted advisor across borders. His network of vetted local partners, from conveyancers in London to escrow officers in Miami, means every transaction runs to one standard: his.

Fluent in the local nuances of each market — foreign-buyer rules in Australia, leasehold structures in the UK, freehold zones in Dubai — James turns cross-border complexity into a clear, confident path to keys in hand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything international buyers ask us — answered plainly

Yes — a large share of our clients purchase sight-unseen. We provide live video walkthroughs, independent building and pest reports, drone footage of the street and surrounds, and a written condition summary before you commit. Your local legal representative reviews every document before exchange.

For buyer representation we charge a fixed engagement fee plus a success fee on settlement, agreed in writing before we begin. In some markets, sell-side commissions offset part of this. There are no hidden referral fees — any partner arrangements are disclosed upfront.

We're not lenders, but we work with mortgage brokers in each market who specialise in non-resident and expat lending. We'll introduce you early so pre-approval is in place before we make offers — often the difference between winning and losing a competitive property.

We attend every shortlisted property in person and run a scheduled live video call from the property, in your time zone. You'll get a recorded walkthrough, our honest notes on condition and street appeal, and comparable sales data — the same brief we'd give a client standing next to us.

Professional fees are payable by bank transfer in AUD, USD, GBP, AED or SAR. Property purchase funds always flow through the regulated channel for that market — trust accounts, escrow, or solicitor client accounts — never through us directly. We can also introduce FX specialists to reduce currency-conversion costs.

As a guide: Australia typically settles in 4–6 weeks from exchange; the US closes in 30–45 days; the UK averages 8–12 weeks due to conveyancing chains; Dubai off-plan can complete in days, with resale transfers around 2–4 weeks. We map your exact timeline in the first consultation.
